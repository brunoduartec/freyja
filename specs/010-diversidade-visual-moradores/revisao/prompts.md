Cada pose foi editada individualmente no gerador de imagens integrado a partir do PNG correspondente em `Referencias/Poses-Moradores/`.

## Instrução comum

Edit the single supplied fictional Freyja character reference. The result will be used as a 3D modeling reference. Substantively redesign the facial geometry and hair of the character as described, along with the tone of exposed skin. Shape cheekbones, nose, eyelids, jaw and hair in the same stylized visual language, with natural individual variation and no caricature. Keep the source's facial stylization, age and mood. Keep the exact original costume, accessories, proportions, A-pose and camera, with relaxed anatomically coherent hands. Keep the original portrait 2:3 frame and the entire figure from top of hair to soles in frame, with the same margins, pale blue-gray background and soft diffuse lighting. Match the reference's matte triangular low-poly faceting on body and face. No text, extra props, added jewelry, ground, or scenery.
